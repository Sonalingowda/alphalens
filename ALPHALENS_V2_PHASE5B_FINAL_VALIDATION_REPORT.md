# AlphaLens v2 Phase 5B Final Validation Report

**Version:** Validation Framework v1.0.0

**Status:** Validation-system framework complete; empirical validation not begun

## 1. Scope and Determination

This report assesses completion of the scientific validation and calibration
framework. It does not report experiment results or approve production policy.
The framework is complete at the specification level and can govern any future
AlphaLens policy through immutable dataset, replay, walk-forward, experiment,
metric, calibration, statistics, governance, promotion, and readiness gates.

### Definitions

`Framework complete` means every requested validation responsibility has a
canonical contract, dependency, failure state, validation method, acceptance
method, and future instantiation gate. `Empirically validated` means a particular
policy has passed a compliant study. No policy currently has that status.

### Assumptions and Dependencies

This determination assumes the frozen Phase 0 through 5A contracts remain
authoritative and immutable evidence can be reproduced. The framework depends
on future approved labels, datasets, experiments, statistical choices, and
human governance before it can evaluate a policy. It assumes no predictive
effect or production suitability.

### Validation Methodology

Framework review MUST verify all required documents, normative terminology,
acyclic dependencies, explicit unavailable states, separation of methodology
from empirical conclusions, and absence of invented production values. The
final document audit performs these checks without executing policy research.

## 3. Remaining Research Gaps

- no approved quantitative label/outcome policy;
- no frozen Phase 5B population, symbols, markets, timeframes, or date range;
- no instantiated market-context/structure definitions;
- no detection, assessment, qualification, score, ranking, plan, lifecycle, or
  notification policy variants;
- no approved confidence estimand;
- no preregistered primary metrics or hypotheses;
- no empirical evidence of practical or statistical validity.

These gaps prevent experiments and production promotion; they do not make the
validation framework incomplete.

## 4. Remaining Calibration Tasks

Future studies must instantiate parameter spaces, objectives, constraints,
nested chronological folds, missing/path rules, sensitivity, uncertainty,
multiplicity, stability, adequacy, and numeric acceptance criteria. Detection,
normalization, weights, ranking, qualification, plans, and notifications require
separate calibration artifacts. Confidence remains absent unless separately
authorized.

## 5. Remaining Implementation Tasks

No software was modified in this phase. Before empirical execution, the
repository still requires contract-compliant implementations or verified reuse
for dataset snapshot construction, generic policy replay, experiment/artifact
storage, protected-test access control, calibration execution, metric/statistical
computation, immutable approvals, research-dashboard projection, and durable
archive/reproduction. Each implementation MUST pass contract and scientific
fixture tests before real studies.

## 6. Statistical Readiness Assessment

The methodology is ready for preregistration. It defines temporal validation,
purge/embargo, nested selection, effect estimation, intervals, multiplicity,
robustness, sensitivity, bias audits, and protected evaluation without choosing
their numeric values.

AlphaLens is not statistically validated: no compliant Phase 5B experiment has
run, no effect has been estimated, no interval or test has been produced, and no
conclusion is supported.

## 7. Production Readiness Assessment

Production activation of opportunity publication, notifications, scores,
rankings, and plans is `NO-GO`. Required policy, empirical, independent-review,
operational, and signed approval evidence does not exist. Existing fail-closed
policy gates MUST remain disabled.

## 8. Risks

Primary risks are label misspecification, temporal leakage, nonstationarity,
multiple testing, researcher degrees of freedom, dependence, missingness,
scope-transfer failure, intrabar ambiguity, protected-test contamination,
metric gaming, and confusing statistical significance with practical value.
The framework requires these risks to be measured and disclosed; it does not
assume they can be eliminated.

## 9. Recommendations and Execution Order

1. Approve one complete quantitative label/outcome policy.
2. Freeze dataset scope, version, lineage, chronology, and protected test.
3. Complete a descriptive data-quality audit before policy experiments.
4. Preregister minimal candidate policies, references, metrics, statistics, and
   acceptance rules.
5. Execute development-only walk-forward experiments and retain all results.
6. Calibrate only through nested chronological selection.
7. Conduct robustness, sensitivity, bias, and independent reproduction.
8. Freeze one candidate and request separate protected-test authorization.
9. Submit the complete evidence dossier to independent promotion review.
10. Keep every production capability disabled unless its exact readiness gates
    and operational controls receive explicit approval.

## 10. Acceptance Methodology and Future Work

Framework acceptance requires document completeness, terminology consistency,
acyclic dependencies, explicit assumptions, no hidden defaults, and no invented
production values or conclusions. Future work is the governed instantiation,
implementation, execution, and review described above; it SHALL create new
versioned artifacts rather than modifying this framework silently.

## 11. Final Status

The complete validation and calibration framework has been produced without
inventing detection thresholds, score weights, confidence mappings, plan
formulas, notification thresholds, or unsupported statistical conclusions.
