# AlphaLens v2 Quantitative Research Final Report

**Version:** Research Specification v1.0.0

**Status:** Final Phase 5A research-framework report

## 0. Scope, Symbols, and Assumptions

This report assesses the research library, not market performance or production
policy. Let \(\mathcal D_R\) be the set of research specifications,
\(\mathcal U\) unresolved calibration quantities, and \(\mathcal G\) the
ordered Phase 5B gates. Completeness means every requested concept has a formal
definition or an explicit member of \(\mathcal U\); it does not mean a quantity
has been empirically supported. The report assumes frozen engineering contracts,
immutable evidence, and the Research Protocol remain authoritative.

## 1. Research Completeness Assessment

The complete policy-neutral mathematical foundation requested for AlphaLens has
been produced. It covers market-state representation, candidate detection,
evidence semantics, opportunity assessment, score interfaces, ranking theory,
plan geometry, lifecycle transitions, deterministic explainability,
assumptions, calibration dependencies, and cross-library review.

The framework preserves deterministic execution, point-in-time correctness,
future isolation, prefix invariance, provenance, explainability, and fail-closed
behavior. It does not modify software or frozen engineering architecture.

## 3. Remaining Unknowns

The following values are intentionally unresolved:

- outcome/label estimand and horizon;
- eligible research populations and scope-transfer rules;
- observation windows and multi-timeframe joins to study;
- trend, momentum, volatility, structure, and session context definitions;
- structure confirmation, equality, break, and state semantics;
- evidence polarity, severity, redundancy, and conflict mappings;
- detection predicates and necessary/sufficient clause sets;
- assessment component functions and stance mapping;
- qualification gates and missing/conflict policy;
- score estimand, components, normalization, weights, aggregation, and scale;
- ranking comparability, freshness use, duplicate relation, and tie key;
- plan reference, entry, invalidation, targets, horizon, and path rules;
- lifecycle freshness, continuation, expiration, invalidation, and renewal;
- explanation templates, locale mappings, and materiality rules;
- any confidence estimand or calibration method;
- runtime notification and API release policies.

These are Phase 5B research/calibration inputs, not omissions to be filled by
implementation judgment.

## 4. Calibration Prerequisites

Before empirical work begins, Phase 5B MUST approve and freeze:

1. a quantitative label policy and canonical outcome semantics;
2. dataset version, eligible scopes, observation membership, and exclusions;
3. chronological folds, purge/embargo, and protected final test;
4. primary/secondary estimands and metrics;
5. candidate mathematical families and finite search spaces;
6. missingness, censoring, intrabar ambiguity, and outlier rules;
7. uncertainty, effect-size, and multiple-comparison procedures;
8. deterministic seeds where applicable and exact preprocessing;
9. acceptance, stopping, stability, and adequacy criteria;
10. immutable configuration, code, prediction, and result hashes.

No protected evidence may be inspected to choose these items.

## 5. Mathematical Readiness

Mathematical readiness is **complete at the research-framework level**. Every
quantity has a type, role, dependency, undefined state, validation obligation,
and calibration gate. Cross-document terminology and dependency direction are
consistent, and no circular definition remains.

Mathematical readiness for production parameterization is **not established**;
it depends on Phase 5B results and approval.

## 6. Statistical Readiness

Statistical methodology is specified at the governance level: chronological
out-of-fold evaluation, protected testing, uncertainty, multiplicity control,
stability analysis, sensitivity, ablation, subgroup analysis, and null/adverse
reporting are required.

Statistical evidence is **not yet available**. No study has been preregistered
or executed under this library, no effect has been estimated, and no predictive
or practical significance is claimed.

## 7. Recommended Phase 5B Work Plan

Phase 5B SHOULD proceed through the following gates without overlap that could
leak protected results upstream:

1. freeze the label/outcome policy and estimand;
2. freeze dataset membership and chronological evaluation protocol;
3. audit source, feature, provenance, missingness, and population coverage;
4. preregister descriptive context and structure studies;
5. preregister evidence redundancy and contradiction studies;
6. evaluate detection hypotheses using development folds only;
7. evaluate assessment and qualification candidates;
8. define and evaluate score candidates only after the quality estimand is
   stable;
9. evaluate cross-scope comparability and ranking only after score semantics;
10. evaluate plan/lifecycle quantities only against approved path and horizon
    semantics;
11. validate deterministic explanation templates independently of predictive
    performance;
12. conduct a formal research review, freeze the selected configuration, and
    request separate approval before one-time protected evaluation;
13. create production policies only through a later explicit governance action.

Confidence research remains a separate optional branch after a valid frozen
decision policy and its required calibration prerequisites exist.

## 8. Risk Assessment

Primary risks are nonstationarity, temporal leakage, multiple testing,
selection bias, evidence dependence, scope-transfer failure, missing-not-at-
random inputs, path ambiguity, and economic overinterpretation. The framework
does not eliminate these risks; it makes them testable, auditable, and capable
of failing closed.

## 9. Final Determination

All requested mathematical research specifications have been produced without
introducing production thresholds, score weights, confidence values, trading
rules, profitability objectives, or unsupported statistical claims.
